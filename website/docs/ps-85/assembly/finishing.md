# Finishing Touches

## Painting and Weathering

I won't go into a ton of detail here, just the general process. For more details, check out OddViking on YouTube, his techniques for weathering stormtrooper armor work well here.

1. Spray the parts with a [metallic silver paint](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000PQGE2M). Let dry.
2. Apply a masking agent (I used toothpaste) to the areas you want to be chipped. Let dry.
3. Spray the parts with a [satin white paint](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B002BWOS8A). Let dry.
4. Scrape off the masking agent with a fingernail and/or brush to reveal the silver underneath. (Easiest to do under running water.)
5. Add additional scratches with 80 grit sandpaper, a file or any other tools.
6. Assemble everything.
7. Apply an acrylic wash. Mix a bit of black and brown acrylic paint with water and carefully brush it onto the case, avoiding getting water on any electronics. Wipe off the excess with a towel. Let dry.
